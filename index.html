Show Me the Monet

Every canvas here is a new Bridge over a Pond of Water Lilies, painted touch by touch from a seed: the arch of the footbridge, the willows, the rafts of lilies and the light they're seen in. Seed paints Monet's own composition.

Drift in to step through the canvas into a rowboat and row on through an endless water garden built of painted cutouts, like a diorama lit from within.

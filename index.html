Show Me the Monet

Every canvas here is a new Bridge over a Pond of Water Lilies, painted touch by touch from a seed: the arch of the footbridge, the willows, the rafts of lilies and the light they're seen in. Monet painted the bridge again and again, in every light; choose the light and the canvas is painted over in another of his series. Seed paints his own composition. The painting is alive: its strokes of paint stream along the pond's currents. Switch on Stir the painting and your cursor stirs them into eddies and leaves a trail of fresh paint; ripples spread, the lilies you brush past send out rings, the flowers glow, and a click splashes.

Step inside and you pass through the canvas into a rowboat and row on through an endless water garden built of painted cutouts, like a diorama lit from within. Branches arch overhead, the hour turns as you row, and now and then there is a boat tied up at a bank, a stone lantern, a footbridge or a sunlit clearing. Make a postcard to frame the canvas, or wherever you have rowed to, with a handwritten caption.

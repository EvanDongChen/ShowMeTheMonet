Show Me the Monet

Every canvas here is a new Bridge over a Pond of Water Lilies, painted touch by touch from a seed: the arch of the footbridge, the willows, the rafts of lilies and the light they're seen in. Monet painted the bridge again and again, in every light; choose the light and the canvas is painted over in another of his series. Seed paints his own composition. The pond is alive. With Live pond on, just move your cursor through it: lilies are planted on the water as you go and grow from buds into blooms, petals scatter and float away, and the dragonfly comes to hover over what you plant. Hold still over the water and the fish come to see. It is all still there when you return. Switch it off and the painting is perfectly still.

Step inside and you pass through the canvas into a rowboat and row on through an endless water garden built of painted cutouts, like a diorama lit from within. Branches arch overhead, the hour turns as you row, and now and then there is a boat tied up at a bank, a stone lantern, a footbridge or a sunlit clearing. Make a postcard to frame the canvas, or wherever you have rowed to, with a handwritten caption.
